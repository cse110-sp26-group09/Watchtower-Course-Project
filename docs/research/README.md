# Research & Discovery

This directory contains research findings, competitive analysis, user personas, and product direction that inform WatchTower's development.

## Purpose

Research helps us understand:
- **User needs** – What problems we're solving and for whom
- **Market landscape** – How competitors approach observability
- **Best practices** – Industry standards and patterns
- **Design opportunities** – UX patterns and workflows that resonate with users

This research informs our requirements, features, dashboard design, and product roadmap.

## Research Areas

### User Personas & Needs

Understand who uses WatchTower and what they need.

- [Woosik Research](Woosik-research.md) – User needs and scenarios
- [Jason Research](Jason-research.md) – Competitive analysis and features
- [Hemendra Research](Hemendra-research.md) – Dashboard design and workflows
- [Waleed Research](Waleed-research.md) – Technical features

### Key Topics Covered

- **Alerting & Uptime Tracking** – How teams monitor system health
- **Dashboard & Analytics UX** – Effective data visualization and navigation
- **Error Tracking & Deployment Monitoring** – Connecting errors to code releases
- **User Needs & Personas** – Developer workflows and pain points

## Using This Research

### During Planning
- Reference user personas when writing requirements
- Use competitive analysis to identify feature gaps
- Consider UX patterns when designing workflows

### During Design
- Follow UX patterns from research
- Ensure dashboard design matches user workflows
- Validate feature prioritization against user needs

### During Development
- Keep user goals in mind when implementing features
- Test assumptions from research
- Document learnings that validate or challenge research

## Contributing Research

When adding new research:

1. **Name clearly** – Use descriptive file names (e.g., `competitor-analysis.md`)
2. **Include date** – Add the research date at the top
3. **Document findings** – What did you learn and why does it matter?
4. **Link to decisions** – Reference this research when it influences decisions
5. **Update decision log** – Link research to decisions in [../meetings/decision-log.md](../meetings/decision-log.md)
