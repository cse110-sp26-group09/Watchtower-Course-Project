## Summary

This research establishes the structural and visual blueprint for a high-converting SaaS landing page designed to maximize user acquisition. Unlike a broad storefront, a landing page operates as a closed funnel that eliminates standard navigation to prevent choice paralysis and keep users focused on a single conversion goal. To successfully entice visitors, our page must pair a high-contrast, persistent "gotcha" button with a left-aligned hero section and clean, contextual product visuals. By utilizing dynamic Single Page Application (SPA) tabs to display complex details like pricing and FAQs, we can deliver a focused, frictionless experience that minimizes cognitive load and drives immediate sign-ups.

---

## Independent Findings
Looking at popular landing pages for other SAAS companies (Google,Adobe,Discord), what I visually observed from looking at them:

- The hero section is often left-aligned with a visualization of what the product looks like (doesn't even have to be actual product, just has to be in the spirt of what the product is)
- Whole website is visually consistant with default visual settings of product
- The 'gotcha' button (button that takes you to signup/buy page) is the most visually striking part of the page that draws your eyes towards it
- The hero section text is often just a tagline, name of product, and a 1 sentence of description of what the product does
- The landing page uses a relative 'SPA' where to not overload consumer's brain with info, keeps important information (like pricing,FAQs, features) in tabs where it changes the landing page but the 'gotcha button' redirects to different page
- There is a sticky header with the 'gotcha button' at all times so the consumer can always access the product

## Research
- Core Purpose: A homepage serves as a broad brand storefront for self-guided exploration, whereas a landing page isolates a single conversion goal designed to drive a specific action.  
- Navigation Architecture: Homepages utilize extensive menus and internal links to help users browse; landing pages eliminate navigation entirely to remove distractions and choice paralysis.  
- Call-to-Action (CTA) 
- Strategy: Homepages present diverse CTAs for various user intents, while landing pages strictly rely on a single, high-contrast CTA repeated strategically down the page.  
- Traffic & Conversion Efficiency: Homepages attract general organic traffic and convert around 2-3%, whereas landing pages are built for targeted paid ads and achieve a much higher median conversion rate of 6.6%. 
-  Optimal Deployment: Deploy homepages to build general brand awareness and organic discovery; deploy landing pages
