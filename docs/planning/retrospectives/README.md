# Sprint Retrospectives

This directory contains retrospective documents for each sprint of the WatchTower project.

## Purpose

Retrospectives are dedicated time for the team to reflect and improve. They help us:

- **Celebrate wins** – Recognize what went well and why
- **Identify blockers** – Understand what prevented progress
- **Share learnings** – Capture insights for the team
- **Drive improvement** – Commit to specific, actionable changes
- **Build team culture** – Foster psychological safety and collaboration

Retrospectives are held at the end of each sprint, typically during the sprint review meeting.

## Format

Each retrospective should capture:

### What Went Well ✨
- Positive aspects of the sprint
- Successes and achievements
- Effective processes or decisions
- Great team moments or collaboration

### What Didn't Go Well 🚧
- Challenges encountered
- Blockers or frustrations
- Process breakdowns
- Incomplete work or scope issues

### Action Items 🎯
- Specific improvements to implement
- Owner and deadline for each item
- How success will be measured
- Connection to sprint goals if relevant

### Lessons Learned 💡
- Key takeaways for the team
- Technical insights or discoveries
- Process improvements to try
- Dependencies or constraints to remember

## Guidelines

- **Be honest and constructive** – Create psychological safety
- **Focus on process, not people** – Improve workflows, not individuals
- **Identify patterns** – Look for recurring issues across sprints
- **Keep it actionable** – Avoid vague complaints; suggest solutions
- **Follow up** – Review action items from previous sprints
- **Celebrate wins** – Don't skip positive reflections

## Facilitating a Retrospective

### Before the Meeting
1. Schedule 60–90 minutes with the team
2. Prepare a template or whiteboard
3. Set a constructive tone in the invitation

### During the Meeting
1. **Check-in** – How is everyone feeling? (2 min)
2. **What went well** – Brainstorm positives (10 min)
3. **What didn't go well** – Identify challenges (15 min)
4. **Root causes** – Why did issues happen? (10 min)
5. **Action items** – What will we try next? (15 min)
6. **Commitments** – Confirm ownership and deadlines (5 min)

### After the Meeting
1. Document findings in this folder
2. Share with the team
3. Reference action items in the next sprint planning
4. Track progress on improvements
